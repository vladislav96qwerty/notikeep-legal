Privacy Policy

NotiKeep: Notification History. Effective date: 19 October 2026.

Developer: Rudoy Vladislav. Contact: notikeep96@gmail.com.

In short

  • NotiKeep keeps a history of your notifications only on this phone.
  • NotiKeep doesn’t send anything from your phone. Your data leaves it only if you export it, create a backup or share it yourself.
  • The developer doesn’t receive your notifications, messages or media copies.
  • NotiKeep has no ads, analytics or crash-reporting tools.

What NotiKeep reads

NotiKeep starts reading only after you tap “Agree and turn on” on the “Access to your notifications” screen and turn on notification access for NotiKeep in Android settings. Until you agree, NotiKeep saves no notifications. Once you agree, NotiKeep also saves the notifications that are in your notification shade at that moment.

NotiKeep reads the content of all notifications, including messages and the names of people who message you, to keep a history and show deleted messages on this phone. It saves notifications only from the apps you choose to keep. So the history also contains other people’s names and messages, group and chat names, and what NotiKeep notices about a message: for example, that it was deleted, edited or dismissed.

If you choose a WhatsApp folder, NotiKeep copies media from it:

  • new photos, videos, voice messages and other WhatsApp media, as they arrive;
  • if you ask, media already in the folder from the last 7 or 30 days;
  • media you send, only if you turn on “Keep what I send”.

NotiKeep doesn’t copy WhatsApp’s private folder or view-once media.

WhatsApp is a trademark of WhatsApp LLC. NotiKeep is not affiliated with or endorsed by WhatsApp.

What NotiKeep stores on this phone

  • Notification history: the app, title, text, sender and chat names, technical identifiers that the app gives to the notification, sender and chat (they may include a phone number or account identifier), time, whether the message was deleted, edited or dismissed, and the earlier text of an edited message.
  • Your choices: favorites, pinned chats, which apps to keep, and settings.
  • Recent searches: the last 10 search queries.
  • Checksums: to notice deleted messages and to keep messages you deleted from NotiKeep from coming back, NotiKeep keeps one-way checksums of those messages and the notification’s technical identifier, not their text, for up to 7 days after the message was last shown in a notification. A checksum can’t be turned back into text; it could only confirm a guess of the exact words, so checksums are kept inside the encrypted history.
  • Copies of WhatsApp media with their file names, and their previews. Also a list of files NotiKeep has already seen in the WhatsApp folder (file names, sizes and dates, not the files), so the same file isn’t copied twice. This list can stay after you clear the history, until you delete WhatsApp’s history in NotiKeep or uninstall NotiKeep.
  • The names of apps that posted notifications, and service information such as counters, the time of the last check and why Android stopped NotiKeep. Service information contains no notification text.
  • Set-aside copies: if the history couldn’t be opened and you chose to start a fresh history, the old encrypted history is set aside in a separate folder on the phone together with its media copies and previews. Media copies that no longer belong to any history are set aside the same way. They stay there until you delete them in Settings → Data → “Set-aside copies of old history”.

How your data is protected

  • The notification history, including recent searches and checksums, is encrypted. The key is protected by the Android Keystore.
  • Media copies and their previews, settings and service information (including the WhatsApp folder you chose) are kept in NotiKeep’s private storage on the phone, which other apps can’t open, but they aren’t separately encrypted.
  • Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for NotiKeep, so Android doesn’t copy your data to Google Drive or to a new phone.
  • If you turn on the NotiKeep lock, your fingerprint, face, PIN, pattern or password is checked by Android. NotiKeep never receives them.
  • NotiKeep’s logs don’t contain notification text.
  • NotiKeep’s own notifications about deleted messages may show the sender’s or chat’s name, unless the NotiKeep lock is on. They never show the message text. Android may keep these notifications in its own notification history.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

  • History is kept until you delete it or until it becomes older than the period you choose in Settings → “Delete older than”. Favorites are never deleted automatically.
  • When you delete a message, a chat, an app’s history or all history in NotiKeep, the related media copies and previews are deleted too. “Delete all media” deletes all media copies.
  • Checksums are deleted 7 days after the message was last shown in a notification.
  • “Withdraw consent” stops saving right away. You can also delete all history, media copies and set-aside copies at the same time.
  • Set-aside copies are kept until you delete them.
  • When you uninstall NotiKeep, Android deletes all its data from the phone, including media copies and set-aside copies. Files you exported, backed up or shared to other apps stay where you saved or sent them.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you do it yourself:

  • Export and backup: you choose what to export and where to save it.
  • “Share” and “Open in another app”: the text or file you select goes to the app you pick.
  • “Copy”: the text you copy goes to Android’s clipboard, marked as sensitive.
  • “Copy details”: NotiKeep copies the phone model, Android version, app version, saving status and service counters (for example, how often Android stopped NotiKeep) to the clipboard, without notification text or names. You decide where to paste them.

After that, the privacy policy of the app or service that receives the data applies.

Internet and third parties

NotiKeep has no internet access. The developer doesn’t sell or share your data and doesn’t receive your notifications, messages or media copies. The developer receives only what you email yourself and the crash statistics from Google Play described below.

Purchases

If you buy NotiKeep Pro, the payment is handled by Google Play under Google’s privacy policy. NotiKeep doesn’t receive your payment details. It only checks on the phone that the purchase is confirmed.

Crash statistics from Android

If you allow your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may show the developer crash and performance reports for NotiKeep (Android vitals), for example the error, phone model and Android version. NotiKeep is built so that its crash reports don’t include notification text.
